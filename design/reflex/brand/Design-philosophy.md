# Measured Pulse

Market activity has rhythm: a quiet baseline, a surge, a reversal, and a recovery. Measured Pulse turns that sequence into a single continuous signature. Its asymmetry gives Reflex an identifiable silhouette without relying on decorative financial symbols.

The line is carefully proportioned around a compact square. A shorter opening and longer recovery create direction, while rounded joins soften the sharp changes in momentum. Precision comes from the relationship between its intervals, stroke weight, and surrounding space.

The lowercase wordmark provides a steady counterweight. Outlined Bricolage Grotesque lettering keeps the identity consistent across environments. The symbol carries energy; the lettering carries clarity.

Aquamarine is the primary signal against Ebony. Foam provides legibility and Firefly supports quieter surfaces. No gradient is needed inside the mark: its crafted geometry remains recognizable in one color, at small sizes, and in reverse.

Allow at least one quarter of the symbol width as clear space. Prefer the symbol alone below 24 pixels; use the complete lockup where the wordmark has room to breathe. Every application should preserve the original proportions and stroke relationships.
